Dear May,

I remember it vividly. I was sitting at my desk, waiting for the bell to ring, scrolling on my phone. I wanted time to move faster so I could go home after spending hours in a place where I barely knew anyone. Little did I know, a simple question would make life a whole lot better.

Ever since that fateful day in English, you have not only changed my life but the lives of so many people. We have laughed, cried, and cared for each other, and words cannot fully describe how grateful I am to have you in my life.

I still can’t believe you’re turning 18. But I know you’ll fit right in, because you’ve been making courageous and loving decisions since you were a kid.

I hope you have a wonderful birthday, and I hope you know how much you are loved. I can’t wait to see what the future holds for you, and I’m so excited to be a part of it.

Love,

Joey
